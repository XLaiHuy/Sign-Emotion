EmotiEffLib facial-expression model
-----------------------------------
The enet_b0_8_best_afew ONNX artifact is from EmotiEffLib repository revision
c018fcf96c79c3bd9184f1c9438fe2095c9f1943. It is distributed under Apache-2.0.
SHA-256: 7aa2ea31c1311f4f8aa9d3fdb085d418dd4e7a48c4b9ed41df8c044f91d0213f
Input: RGB face crop, 224x224, ImageNet channel normalization, NCHW float32.
Its eight output logits are mapped to SignEmo's stable label order in the local
worker. The pinned upstream license text is in public/licenses/emotiefflib-Apache-2.0.txt.
