name: signemo-accessible-ui
description: Design or revise SignEmo Deaf-first captions, navigation, typography and light motion while preserving local-data explanations.
---

Read `doc/DESIGN.md`. Captions are primary; emotional suggestions are secondary and uncertain. Use self-hosted Be Vietnam Pro with Vietnamese glyphs, ivory/moss/graphite tokens, body 16–18 px, adjustable captions 20–36 px and touch targets at least 44 px.

Keep three primary destinations: conversation, history and rhythm practice. Settings/lab/admin/feedback are secondary. Do not rely on color or sound; label permission, listening, processing, overload and unknown states. Never auto-scroll away from text the user is reading.

Use subtle 140–180 ms state changes and respect reduced motion. Avoid decorative AI hero graphics and crowded model numbers in the normal flow. Diagnostics belong behind disclosure. Review keyboard focus, 200% text, Vietnamese labels, contrast and small-screen overflow in the actual browser.
