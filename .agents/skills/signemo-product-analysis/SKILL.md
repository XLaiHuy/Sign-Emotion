Read `doc/MVP_SPEC.md`, then relevant parts of `doc/PROJECT_STATUS.md` and `doc/PILOT.md` from the repo root. Distinguish source claims, measured evidence, hypotheses and decisions. The six original documents contain conflicting audio/micro-expression plans and unsupported benchmarks; do not promote them to requirements.

Default audience is consenting adults in 1–1 conversations. Ask the Deaf participants what is useful rather than assuming they cannot recognize faces. Evaluate added benefit over unaided observation. Keep user value, model accuracy and willingness to pay as separate hypotheses.

When recommending a product change, state the affected workflow, measurable outcome, evidence gap and minimum next experiment. Verify current technical/commercial source claims from authoritative sources. Reconcile pricing arithmetic, personnel and cost assumptions before reusing financial projections. Update the decision/evidence ledger when implementing an authorized document change. Do not expand into audio, minors, wearables or payment implicitly.
